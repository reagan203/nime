How a two-year programme runs

Five workstreams (awareness, use-case discovery, implementation, governance and institutionalisation) delivered in four phases.

Months 1–6

Foundation & awareness

  • AI Edge Series and awareness sessions
  • Department-wise process understanding
  • Draft AI usage, governance and data classification frameworks
  • Form the AI Steering Committee and identify AI champions
Months 7–12

Pilot & capability

  • Hackathons and use-case workshops
  • Proofs of concept and pilots
  • SOP documentation and process refinement
  • First knowledge base, AI agents and bridge tools
Months 13–18

Scale & integration

  • Scale successful pilots
  • Integrate AI tools with existing systems
  • Launch the internal AI portal
  • Expand private / offline LLM use on approved documents
Months 19–24

Institutionalise & improve

  • Establish the AI Centre of Excellence
  • Embed AI into SOPs, reporting, training and reviews
  • Track adoption across functions
  • Review AI policy, governance and data controls

People stay in charge

AI assists decision-making; it does not replace approval authority. Sensitive outputs are reviewed by the person responsible, and human approval is mandatory for financial decisions, legal matters, employee matters, external communication, regulatory submissions and high-risk operational decisions.

Data is classified before it meets AI

Public
Public and internal AI tools
Internal
Approved internal AI tools; not public tools without approval
Confidential
Internal or private AI tools only, under access controls
Restricted
Private or offline AI tools only, with prior approval
Highly sensitive
No AI tool without explicit senior approval and dedicated safeguards

AI Steering Committee

Management, IT, finance, operations, risk, HR, compliance and the AI team approve priority use cases and policies, and review risk and adoption.

AI Champions

One or two per department or company, driving awareness, collecting use cases and supporting training.

Centre of Excellence

Keeps reusable templates, prompts, SOPs and standards, so capability does not depend on individuals.
