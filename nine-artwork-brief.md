# Artwork brief: "Nine", the Ninth Mind assistant

Ninth Mind Limited, Nairobi, Kenya · www.ninthmind.ai

## What we need

Nine is the animated assistant on the Ninth Mind website. It walks along the page, waves, sits, sleeps, dances and talks to visitors. We already have the animation working with a simple drawn body. We need a professional illustration of the **body** in a **realistic anime style**, delivered as separate parts that our animation rig moves.

The **head is not part of this job.** Nine keeps its existing robot head: a rounded white head with a dark face screen, glowing eyes, blue ear pods and an antenna topped with a blue sphere. Please draw the body so that head sits naturally on it (screenshot below to be supplied).

## The character

- **Who:** an original adult man, slim and athletic, standing upright, facing the viewer.
- **Style:** realistic anime. Clean line art, cel shading with two shadow tones, light from the top left, realistic adult proportions (about 8 heads tall).
- **Clothes:** a tailored navy suit (jacket and trousers), white shirt with collar, a tie in Ninth Mind blue, a light-blue pocket square, and a small round light-blue lapel pin. Polished black shoes.
- **Skin (hands only):** warm medium-brown.

## Originality and rights (please read)

- The design must be **entirely original**. Do not base it on, trace or reference any existing character, film, game or anime, including *Alita: Battle Angel*, and do not use the likeness of any real person.
- No AI-generated imagery unless we agree it in writing first.
- On payment, the artist **assigns all copyright** in the artwork and source files to **Ninth Mind Limited**, waives moral rights to the extent the law allows, and confirms the work is original and infringes no one's rights.

## Parts to deliver

Draw each part **straight, hanging vertically**, as it would look with the arms and legs at rest. Each part goes in its own transparent PNG at **exactly** the size below. Joints must have **rounded, overlapping ends** (shoulder, elbow, hip, knee) so no gap shows when the part rotates.

Draw the **left-side parts only** (the figure's right as you look at it is mirrored automatically). Pivot points are measured in pixels from the top-left corner of each image.

| File | Size (px) | Pivot (rotates around) | What attaches |
|---|---|---|---|
| `torso.png` | 1320 × 1680 | — | Shirt collar and tie at top centre. Left shoulder pivot at (60, 180), right shoulder at (1260, 180). Hips at (430, 1720) and (890, 1720), just below the jacket hem. |
| `upper-arm.png` | 240 × 800 | (120, 100) | Elbow at (130, 780) |
| `forearm.png` | 220 × 820 | (120, 100) | Include the white shirt cuff at the bottom. Hand attaches at (110, 760). |
| `hand.png` | 240 × 320 | top centre | Relaxed open hand, thumb towards the body |
| `thigh.png` | 300 × 1080 | (150, 120) | Knee at (150, 960) |

Nice to have: a waving hand, a fist, and a seated thigh (seen from the front, foreshortened, 300 × 200) for the sitting pose.

## Files

- Transparent PNGs named exactly as above, plus layered source files (PSD, Procreate or SVG).
- One flat image of the assembled figure in the rest pose, with the robot head placed on top, for approval.

## How we will check it

We drop the PNGs into `assets/nine/` on the website and switch the artwork on. Nine should then walk, wave, sit, sleep, dance and talk with the new body, and no joints should show gaps.
